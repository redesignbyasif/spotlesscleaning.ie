The story

Small on purpose.
Spotless by habit.

Spotless Cleaning started in Drogheda in 2011 with no investment and a simple frustration: too many “cleaners” were leaving the difficult parts untouched. Word travelled. Within a year we were the crew called for the heavy, awkward jobs — and we invested every bit of it back into better equipment.

Equipment is only half of it. The people doing the work matter more, and we only keep the ones who genuinely like it. That is why so much of our week is repeat customers rather than adverts.
